Boards want a number on AI's return, and engineering leaders are the ones expected to produce it. The pressure repeats every quarter: prove the investment is working, prove the team is producing more, prove it in terms finance will accept. The numbers already on hand — deployment counts, story points, AI adoption rates — rarely survive the follow-up question.
